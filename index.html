05 FAQ

Questions

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. On inbound and outbound calls, the agent introduces itself as an assistant for your shop. We do not hide it.

Does RSRS handle inbound calls too?

Yes. It works both ways. It can answer calls coming into your shop, answer common questions, and offer open appointment times. It also makes outbound follow up calls on declined repairs.

Which shop management systems work with RSRS?

We are building around Tekmetric first. If you use something else, tell us and we will tell you honestly whether we can support it.

What does the free pilot include?

We run RSRS on your last 30 days of declined repairs so you can see how the calls go before you pay anything. You approve the script first, and you can listen to the calls.

Can I review the script before calls go out?

Always. Nothing runs until you approve the wording.

Is RSRS live in my area?

We are launching in phases with independent shops around Houston. Request a free pilot and we will walk through where things stand.
